# Doom Tizen v1.21

- Restore the freely redistributable shareware `doom1.wad` (episode 1 only) as the bundled game data.
- Remove Chex Quest support (`chex.wad` and its setting), as it has no redistribution licence.
- Improve controller support.

Ensure your Samsung TV is running Tizen OS version 5.5 or higher. Use
`Doom.wgt` for newer TVs and `Doom-tizen55.wgt` for 2020 TVs running Tizen 5.5.
